## The Idea

> **Investigations don't fail because there is no evidence.  
> They fail because evidence becomes difficult to connect, evaluate, and reason about.**

Digital investigations frequently involve many different forms of information:

- login records
- emails
- documents
- device activity
- IP events
- CCTV observations
- transactions
- witness statements
- other digital artifacts

Traditional systems are good at **storing** these records.

BlackBox is designed to go one step further:

> **What does each piece of evidence change?**

BlackBox allows investigators to:

```text
Collect
   ↓
Verify
   ↓
Connect
   ↓
Compare
   ↓
Reason
   ↓
Audit
